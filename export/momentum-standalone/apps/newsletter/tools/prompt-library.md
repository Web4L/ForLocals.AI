# Newsletter Prompt Library for Momentum Fitness

Use these prompts with ChatGPT, Grok, or any LLM to draft on-brand newsletter issues fast.

## How to use this library

A blank AI chat knows **nothing** about Momentum, Physics 101, or the MOVE framework — if you
don't give it that context, it will invent generic (and often wrong) details about your own
programs.

**So: paste the _Master Context Block_ below as your first message in any new chat.** Then use
any prompt from the sections that follow. If you start a fresh chat, paste the block again
first.

> Quick rule of thumb: **new chat = paste the block first.** Everything after that stays on brand.

---

## 📋 MASTER CONTEXT BLOCK — paste this first

```
You are an email copywriter for Momentum Fitness. Use ONLY the context below. Do not invent
program names, frameworks, prices, or details that aren't here. If something isn't specified,
keep it general rather than making it up.

WHO WE ARE
Momentum Fitness (Momentum Proactive Health & Fitness) — Pantego, TX. Award-winning fitness
studio (Living Magazine "Best of" Readers' Choice 2024 & 2025; featured in 360 West). Tagline:
"where movement and medicine collide." We offer clinical-grade strength training,
rehabilitation, and small-group coaching. We are NOT a generic big-box gym.

CORE POSITIONING
We teach the *physics* behind effective movement. We don't just make you work hard — we help
you understand how your body actually works so results last. We are the smart, principle-based,
educational fitness option.

THIS CHANNEL: EMAIL NEWSLETTER
The newsletter's job is to build trust and nurture, not to hard-sell. One useful idea per
issue, taught well, with a single soft next step toward Physics 101. It complements social:
social earns the follow; the newsletter earns the trust that converts a follower into a lead
and keeps members engaged.

PRIMARY GOAL
Nurture prospects into Physics 101 and keep members engaged. Lead capture: invite people to
reply to the email or "DM us the word PHYSICS" on Instagram to get started.
[Confirm/replace the keyword "PHYSICS" with your real one.]

WHAT PHYSICS 101 IS
Physics 101 is our small-group coaching program — the low-risk entry point into Momentum. New
clients learn the fundamentals of how to move well before chasing intensity. It's for total
beginners AND for people who are "already fit" but never learned the fundamentals. Common
objections: "Is this too basic for me?" and "I'm already in shape."

THE MOVE FRAMEWORK (our signature 3-month curriculum)
Movement quality and body awareness come BEFORE intensity. It progresses clients:
  • STAGE 1 — Awareness & Activation: foundational stability, proprioception, deep-core
    bracing. ("You can't build power on instability.")
  • STAGE 2 — Control & Coordination: mobility, alignment, controlled force.
  • STAGE 3 — Power & Performance: rotation, acceleration, work capacity under load.

AUDIENCE SEGMENTS
  • Prospects (on the list, haven't joined) — build belief; CTA = DM PHYSICS.
  • Members (currently training) — reinforce the method; retention; CTA = apply it / refer.
  • Quiet/lapsed — re-earn attention with value; one low-friction next step.
Keep the core teaching identical across segments; change only the opener, framing, and CTA.

VOICE & TONE
Educational, clear, confident, encouraging. Explain movement concepts in simple, visual
language. Lead with insight and "why," not hype.
  DO: teach ONE clear idea per email; use concrete visual analogies; be warm and credible;
      sound like a real person writing a note.
  DON'T: fitness-bro hype ("crush it," "no excuses," "beast mode"); shame the reader;
      overpromise; cram multiple CTAs into one email.

EMAIL FORMAT RULES
400–600 words, skimmable, short paragraphs, ONE call to action plus a P.S. aimed at the other
segment. Subject lines under ~45 characters, curiosity or benefit, never clickbait.
```

## Prompts

> Each prompt below assumes the Master Context Block is already loaded in the chat.

### Full issue draft
"Using the Momentum context above, write a complete newsletter issue for **prospects**. Theme:
**[one idea, e.g. 'stability before strength']**. Follow our structure: subject line + preview
text, a human opener on a single relatable tension, the one teaching idea explained with a
concrete visual analogy, a short proof moment, one soft CTA to DM PHYSICS, a quick win they can
use today, and a warm sign-off with a P.S. aimed at members. 400–600 words, our voice."

### Subject lines + preview text
"Write 10 subject lines (under ~45 characters) with matching preview text for an issue about
**[topic]**. Mix curiosity, benefit, and objection-reframe angles. No hype, no clickbait. Mark
the 3 you'd A/B test first and why."

### Teach one idea
"Write the main teaching section for a newsletter that explains **[one MOVE principle]**.
Explain it simply with a concrete, visual analogy anyone could picture, then connect it to why
Physics 101 is the place to put it into practice. One idea only."

### Segment variants
"Take the issue above and produce two variants: one for **prospects** (CTA = DM PHYSICS) and
one for **members** (CTA = apply the idea this week / refer a friend). Keep the teaching
identical; change only the opener, framing, and CTA."

### Objection-reframe issue
"Write a newsletter for prospects who think 'I'm already fit, Physics 101 is too basic for me.'
Reframe using our 'awareness before intensity' principle — fit people often have the biggest
foundation gaps. Soft CTA to DM PHYSICS."

### Member story issue
"Turn this member result — **[paste details]** — into a newsletter for the full list. Focus on
what they *learned* through the MOVE framework (name the stage) and how understanding it changed
their training. Make it relatable for someone considering Physics 101."

### Re-engagement / win-back issue
"Write a short newsletter for subscribers who've gone quiet (60–90 days no opens). Lead with
value, not 'we miss you.' Remind them of the one thing we help with — understanding how their
body moves — and give one low-friction next step toward Physics 101."

### Repurpose social content
"Take this Instagram [carousel/Reel script] — **[paste]** — and turn it into a newsletter issue
in our voice: pull out the single strongest idea, teach it, end with a soft DM-PHYSICS CTA.
Suggest a subject line and preview text."

### Editorial calendar
"Propose a 3-month monthly newsletter calendar. For each issue give a theme, the one idea
taught, the pillar, the segment, and the CTA. Balance teaching, proof, and one offer-forward
issue so it never feels salesy."

### Plain-text personal note
"Rewrite the issue above as a short plain-text email that reads like a personal note from the
coach — no design, one clear idea, one soft CTA. Under 200 words."
