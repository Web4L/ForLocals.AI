# Content Pillars for Momentum Fitness

**Core Positioning**: Momentum is the gym that teaches you the *physics* behind effective movement. We don't just make you work hard — we make you understand how your body actually works so results last.

**Primary Objective**: Drive qualified new leads into **Physics 101** classes while building long-term brand authority as the smart, principle-based fitness option.

## Pillar 1: The Physics of Movement (Educational Authority)
**Goal**: Establish Momentum as the intelligent choice.
- Explain movement principles simply and visually
- Break down concepts from the MOVE framework (stability, control, power)
- Show why most generic programs fall short
- Best formats: Educational Carousels + Reels

## Pillar 2: Physics 101 Experience & Journey
**Goal**: Make Physics 101 the clear, low-risk entry point.
- Show exactly what happens in Physics 101 classes
- Share student progress and "aha" moments
- Address objections ("Is this too basic?", "I'm already fit")
- Content that reduces friction for new leads

## Pillar 3: Proof & Transformations
**Goal**: Build trust through real results (especially from Physics 101 starters).
- Client stories and specific outcomes
- Before/after with context (what they learned)
- Testimonials focused on understanding + results

## Pillar 4: Culture & Expertise
**Goal**: Humanize the brand and showcase depth.
- Trainer philosophy and expertise
- Behind-the-scenes of how principles are applied
- Community and environment at Momentum

## Pillar 5: Offers & Entry Points
**Goal**: Convert interest into action toward Physics 101.
- Physics 101-specific offers and intros
- Educational lead magnets
- Clear next steps for interested followers

**Platform Strategy**:
- **Instagram (Primary)**: Reels for reach + Carousels for education. Strong focus on Physics 101 content.
- **TikTok**: Short, punchy educational clips + authentic day-in-the-life.
- **Facebook**: Longer-form educational content + community building.
