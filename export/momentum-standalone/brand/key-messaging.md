# Key Messaging Framework

## Core Brand Message
Momentum teaches you the physics of movement so your results actually last.

## Positioning Statement
For people who are tired of guessing their way through training, Momentum is the gym that gives you a real foundation through Physics 101 — so you stop wasting time on programs that don't work long-term.

## Physics 101 Messaging Angles

**Primary Angle**: "The missing foundation most people skip"
**Secondary Angles**:
- "Train smarter, not just harder"
- "Build results that actually stick"
- "Understand *why* you're doing what you're doing"

## Target Audience for Physics 101 Leads
- People who have trained before but feel stuck or inconsistent
- Those who want to do things properly instead of following random workouts
- People who value understanding over quick fixes
- Slightly more mature or thoughtful fitness audience

## Objections to Address
- "I'm already pretty fit — do I really need this?"
- "Isn't this just for beginners?"
- "Will this actually help me get stronger/fitter?"
- "How is this different from normal gym classes?"
