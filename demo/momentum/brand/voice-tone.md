# Voice & Tone Guidelines

**Brand Voice**: Intelligent, clear, confident, and encouraging — never condescending or overly "bro".

**Key Traits**:
- Educational but accessible
- Precise (we talk about physics and principles, not just "feel the burn")
- Supportive of the journey (especially new clients)
- Direct when explaining why most training falls short

**Do**:
- Use simple explanations for complex movement concepts
- Reference principles from the MOVE framework
- Focus on long-term understanding over quick fixes

**Don't**:
- Use generic fitness platitudes
- Overpromise rapid transformations without context
- Talk down to beginners
