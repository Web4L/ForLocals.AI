# Issue Structure Template

The repeatable skeleton for every Momentum newsletter. Copy this, fill the brackets, and you
have a draft. The rule that makes it work: **one idea per issue, taught well.**

---

**Subject line:** [under ~45 characters — curiosity or benefit, never clickbait]
**Preview text:** [one line that extends the subject, not repeats it]

---

Hi [First Name],

**1. Opener (2–4 lines).**
Open on a single relatable tension your reader actually feels. Sound like a person, not a
brand. No "Welcome to our newsletter!" — earn attention from the first line.

**2. The one idea (the core).**
Teach ONE principle — usually from the MOVE framework (stability → control → power). Explain
it with a concrete, visual example anyone could picture (the house/foundation, the weak link,
etc.). Lead with *why*. Resist the urge to add a second and third idea.

> Optional pull-quote: state the principle in one plain sentence. These are eminently
> screenshot-able and reinforce the teaching.

**3. Proof (short).**
A member moment, result, or "aha." Keep it specific and human. Anonymize unless you have a
yes to use the name.

**4. Soft CTA (one, only one).**
Point to Physics 101 with no pressure. Reply to the email OR DM **PHYSICS** on Instagram.
Frame it as the place to *put the idea into practice*, not as a hard sell.

**5. Quick win.**
A practical tip or PRACTICE habit they can use today — gives value even if they never click.

Sign-off,
[Coach Name]
Momentum Fitness · Pantego, TX

*P.S. — [Use the P.S. for the secondary segment. If the email is written to prospects, the
P.S. speaks to members — and vice versa. People read the P.S.]*

---

## Length & format guardrails
- **400–600 words.** Long enough to teach, short enough to finish.
- Skimmable: short paragraphs, one idea per paragraph, generous white space.
- One CTA. Competing asks kill conversion.
- Plain and warm beats designed and corporate — a personal-note feel converts.

## Voice checklist (before you hit send)
- [ ] Teaches exactly one idea.
- [ ] Explains it simply and visually (no jargon dumps).
- [ ] Leads with *why*, not hype.
- [ ] No fitness-bro language ("crush it," "beast mode," "no excuses").
- [ ] Doesn't shame the reader or overpromise.
- [ ] PHYSICS keyword is the live one.
- [ ] Exactly one CTA + a P.S. for the other segment.
