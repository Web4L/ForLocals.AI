# Subject-Line Library

Swipe file of on-brand subject lines for Momentum. The brand rule: **curiosity or benefit,
never clickbait, never hype.** Pair each with preview text that *extends* the subject rather
than repeating it. Keep subjects under ~45 characters so they don't truncate on mobile.

## Curiosity (teach-forward)
- The step almost everyone skips
- Why you stopped seeing progress
- Your knees probably aren't the problem
- The physics of a plateau
- What your body does when you're not looking
- Strong, but on a shaky base?
- The boring fix that actually works

## Benefit (what's in it for them)
- Train smarter, not just harder
- Make your results actually last
- Stop guessing your way through workouts
- The fastest way past a sticking point
- Build a base that doesn't crack

## Objection-reframe (great for prospects)
- "I'm already fit — do I need this?"
- Too basic for you? Read this first
- Not just for beginners
- Why fit people start with Physics 101

## Story / proof
- He'd been stuck for a year. Then this.
- The member who "just had bad knees"
- One pattern changed everything

## Offer-forward (use sparingly, during a push)
- Your spot in Physics 101
- A smarter place to start
- Ready when you are

## Testing notes
- Test **one variable at a time** (e.g. curiosity vs. benefit angle), not five.
- The subject's only job is the open; the *email's* job is the click. Don't overpromise in the
  subject and underdeliver in the body — it trains the list to ignore you.
- Log winners in `../analytics/` and fold the angle back into the prompt library.
