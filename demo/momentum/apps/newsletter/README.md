# Momentum Fitness — Newsletter

The email newsletter engine for **Momentum Fitness** (Pantego, TX). A recurring,
educational email that teaches the *physics of movement*, nurtures leads who aren't ready to
commit yet, keeps current members engaged, and points everyone toward **Physics 101** (lead
capture via the keyword **PHYSICS**).

**Primary objective:** turn the email list into the highest-ROI channel Momentum owns —
nurture prospects into Physics 101 and re-engage quiet subscribers — while reinforcing brand
authority as the smart, principle-based fitness option.

## What's here
- `index.html` — the dashboard. Open it to browse everything in one styled view.
- `strategy.md` — cadence, audience segments, issue anatomy, and the nurture logic.
- `issues/` — ready-to-send newsletter issues (full copy: subject, preview, body).
- `templates/` — the reusable issue structure + a subject-line library.
- `calendar/` — a 3-month editorial calendar template with themes per issue.
- `tools/prompt-library.md` — copy-paste AI prompts with a Master Context Block.
- `analytics/` — track opens, clicks, replies, and PHYSICS DMs per issue.

## Brand context
This app reads the business's shared brand context from `../../brand/`:
`voice-tone.md`, `key-messaging.md`, `content-pillars.md`, and `move-framework.md`.
Those are the single source of truth — don't duplicate brand details here.

## Why newsletter (the case)
Email is the channel a business actually owns — no algorithm in between. For a
nurture-and-retention play it's among the highest-ROI automations a local business can run.
For Momentum specifically, it's the natural complement to social: social earns the follow,
the newsletter earns the trust that converts a follower into a Physics 101 lead, and keeps
members from quietly drifting away. See `../../../../docs/automation-research.md`.
