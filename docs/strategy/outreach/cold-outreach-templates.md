# Cold Outreach Templates — Social Content Hook

> Part of the GTM playbook (`../go-to-market.md`). The motion is **gift-first**:
> you've already made them free finished work before you ever message them. Lead
> with the gift, not a pitch. Never lead with "AI."

## Rules that make cold work

1. **Show finished work first.** The sample (from `prospect-sample-prompt.md`) is
   the opener. You're not asking for time — you're handing them something.
2. **No jargon, no "AI," no "automation."** Owners care about the result
   ("posts in your voice, done for you"), not the tech.
3. **Tiny, specific ask.** One soft line about $99/mo, cancel anytime. Don't
   stack asks.
4. **Match the channel to the business.** IG/FB DM for the social-active; email
   (from their site) for the rest; in-person works too.
5. **Reference something real** about *their* business so it's obviously not a
   blast.

---

## Instagram / Facebook DM (shortest — best for social-active owners)

```
Hey [First name] — I love what [Business] is doing in [City]. I actually made
you a week of posts in your brand's voice as a free sample (no catch): [link/attach].

If they're useful, I can keep this going for you every week — $99/mo, cancel
anytime. Either way they're yours to use. 🙌
```

## Email (for owners with a site but light socials)

```
Subject: made you a week of posts for [Business] (free)

Hi [First name],

I put together a week of social posts for [Business] — written in your voice,
ready to post. No charge, no catch: [link/attach].

I noticed [one specific, real observation — e.g. "your last post was a while
back" / "your Physics 101 offer would do great as a carousel"], so I figured I'd
just show you instead of pitch you.

If you'd like this every week, I run it for $99/mo, cancel anytime. And if not,
keep these — they're yours.

[Your name]
```

## Follow-up (one, 3–4 days later — only if no reply)

```
Hey [First name] — just making sure the free posts reached you: [link].
No pressure at all. If you'd ever want a steady stream of these, I'm here ($99/mo,
cancel anytime).
```

## On a reply ("interested" / "tell me more")

- Confirm fit in plain English: *"$99/mo, I send you a week of on-brand posts
  every week, you approve and post (or I can post for you once you're set up).
  Cancel anytime."*
- For warm/keen prospects, raise the wow: *"Want me to set you up a little
  dashboard so it all lives in one place?"* → build it with
  `warm-dashboard-howto.md`.
- Don't oversell ROI. The honest pitch is **"consistent, on-brand presence
  without you lifting a finger,"** not "this will grow your sales."

## On a "no" / no reply
Leave the free posts with them. They're a seed — value delivered builds the brand
even when they don't convert today. Don't burn the relationship chasing.
