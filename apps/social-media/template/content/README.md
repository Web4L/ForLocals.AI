# Content

Ready-to-post social content for {{BUSINESS_NAME}}, organized by campaign or theme. Each piece
should educate *and* funnel toward {{OFFER}}.

## How to use
1. Generate content with `../tools/prompt-library.md` (paste the Master Context Block first).
2. Save batches here (e.g. `content/<campaign>/instagram-batch-1.md`).
3. Schedule them on the `../calendar/` and track top performers.

> Add your first content batches here and list the key ones in `../manifest.json` under
> "Content".
