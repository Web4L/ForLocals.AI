# Newsletter Strategy

How {{BUSINESS_NAME}}'s newsletter earns trust and drives {{OFFER}} leads — without feeling
like a sales blast. One useful idea per issue, taught well, with a soft next step.

## Cadence
- **Default: monthly.** Sustainable at a high quality bar; frequent enough to stay top-of-mind.
- Send on a consistent day (e.g. the first Tuesday, mid-morning). Consistency beats timing.

## Audience segments
Keep the core teaching identical across segments; change only the opener, framing, and CTA.

| Segment | Who they are | Goal | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Prospects** | On the list, haven't bought | Build belief; lower friction | DM **{{CTA_KEYWORD}}** |
| **Customers** | Currently active | Reinforce value; retention | Apply it / refer a friend |
| **Quiet / lapsed** | Haven't opened in 60–90 days | Re-earn attention with value | One low-friction next step |

## Issue anatomy
See `templates/issue-structure.md`. In short: subject + preview → human opener → one taught
idea → short proof → one soft CTA toward {{OFFER}} → quick win → warm sign-off.

## Measuring it
Track per issue in `analytics/`: open rate, click rate, replies, and **{{CTA_KEYWORD}} leads
attributable to the send**. The last one is the number that matters.
