# Issue Structure Template

The repeatable skeleton for every {{BUSINESS_NAME}} newsletter. Copy this, fill the brackets,
and you have a draft. The rule that makes it work: **one idea per issue, taught well.**

---

**Subject line:** [under ~45 characters — curiosity or benefit, never clickbait]
**Preview text:** [one line that extends the subject, not repeats it]

---

Hi [First Name],

**1. Opener (2–4 lines).** Open on a single relatable tension. Sound like a person.

**2. The one idea.** Teach ONE principle with a concrete, visual example anyone can picture.
Lead with *why*. Resist adding a second and third idea.

> Optional pull-quote: state the principle in one plain sentence.

**3. Proof (short).** A customer moment, result, or "aha." Specific and human.

**4. Soft CTA (one, only one).** Point to {{OFFER}} with no pressure (reply, or DM
**{{CTA_KEYWORD}}**). Frame it as the place to *put the idea into practice*.

**5. Quick win.** A practical tip they can use today — value even if they never click.

Sign-off,
[Owner / Coach Name]
{{BUSINESS_NAME}} · {{LOCATION}}

*P.S. — Use the P.S. for the other segment.*

---

## Guardrails
- **400–600 words.** Skimmable, short paragraphs, one CTA.
- Plain and warm beats designed and corporate.
- Before sending: one idea only · explained simply · leads with *why* · no hype · the
  **{{CTA_KEYWORD}}** keyword is live.
