# Subject-Line Library

Swipe file of on-brand subject lines for {{BUSINESS_NAME}}. The rule: **curiosity or benefit,
never clickbait, never hype.** Pair each with preview text that *extends* the subject. Keep
subjects under ~45 characters so they don't truncate on mobile.

## Curiosity
- [The step almost everyone skips]
- [What most people get wrong about <topic>]

## Benefit
- [Get <result> without <pain>]
- [The faster way to <outcome>]

## Objection-reframe
- ["<common objection>?" — answered]

## Story / proof
- [How <customer> went from <before> to <after>]

---

## Testing notes
- Test **one variable at a time** (curiosity vs. benefit), not five.
- The subject's only job is the open; the email's job is the click. Don't overpromise.
- Log winners in `../analytics/` and fold the angle back into the prompt library.
