The four leaks

You're not losing customers. You're losing them at the seams.

Leak 01 · Missed calls & slow response

The lead hires whoever answers first.

Respond within five minutes and you're ~100× more likely to reach a lead and far more likely to qualify them. Most owners can't — they're with a customer, on a ladder, mid-session. The lead doesn't wait.

Leak 02 · No-shows & cancellations

An empty slot you can never sell twice.

No-shows cost the typical local business 5–7% of gross revenue. Simple SMS reminders cut them by ~34% — one of the highest-ROI messages you can send.

Leak 03 · Silent churn

They don't quit. They just fade.

A 5% lift in retention can mean 25–95% more profit. Selling to an existing customer is 60–70% likely; to a new one, 5–20%. The fade is invisible until the revenue drops.

Leak 04 · Slow follow-up

The quote that went cold.

Firms that cut quote-and-follow-up response from hours to under an hour saw 20%+ revenue gains. Speed isn't a nicety — it's the sale.

Figures from peer-reviewed and industry research compiled in our automation brief; exact impact varies by business.
