**Hook (slide 1):** "3 noises your car makes right before a brake job gets expensive 🛑"
**Caption:**
Brakes don't usually fail out of nowhere — they warn you first. Listen for:
1️⃣ **Squealing** when you slow down → pads getting thin.
2️⃣ **Grinding** → metal on metal. Don't wait on this one.
3️⃣ **A soft or low pedal** → could be fluid or worn pads.
Catch it early and it's a small fix. Wait, and you're into rotors too.
