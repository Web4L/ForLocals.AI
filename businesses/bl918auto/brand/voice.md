# Voice & Tone — BL918AUTO

> Family-owned Tulsa exhaust, muffler & brake shop (B&L Muffler & Brake), in the
> middle of a **re-brand to BL918AUTO**. Voice is built from what their real
> reviews already say: *honest, fast, fair, friendly.*

## Who we are
B&L has been on W 41st in Tulsa since **1989**. Owner **Bill** and crew are known
for straight talk, quick turnaround, fair prices — and **custom exhaust** work
(cat-back systems, the good rumble). The `918` in the new name is Tulsa's area
code: this is a local shop, for local people.

## Voice (do)
- **Plainspoken & honest.** Talk like a trustworthy mechanic, not a marketer. No
  jargon, no hype.
- **Confident, blue-collar, friendly.** A little humor is welcome. Emoji okay in
  moderation (🛠️ 🔊 🧡).
- **Local & proud.** Lean into Tulsa / 918 / "same corner since '94."
- **Teach, don't sell.** Explain what a noise means or what an honest quote looks
  like — earn the call.
- **Show the people.** Real photos of the shop, the bay, Bill, the crew beat
  stock images every time.

## Voice (don't)
- Don't invent prices, discounts, stats, or awards. Everything must survive Bill
  reading it.
- Don't overpromise or fearmonger ("your car will explode!"). Honest > dramatic.
- Don't sound corporate or generic. If it could be any shop in America, rewrite it.

## Call to action
This is a **walk-in shop**, not a DM-keyword funnel. Use soft, real CTAs:
- "Swing by — 1112 W 41st."
- "Call us: (918) 445-5160."
- "Hearing that noise? Bring it in, we'll tell you straight."
- During the campaign: **"See you June 20, 11–2 🍖."**

## ⚠️ Brand assets — placeholder
The accent color (`#e23b2e`, a confident automotive red) and fonts are a
**reasonable placeholder pending B&L's actual re-brand palette/logo.** Drop the
new logo in `brand/assets/` and update `brand.json` `colors`/`logo` once the
