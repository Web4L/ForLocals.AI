# The MOVE Framework

Momentum's signature 3-month client curriculum. This is core brand context — it belongs to
the business and is read by every app (social, ads, newsletter, etc.), not just one.

**Formula:** Rx¹² + p = v → a structured **Prescription**, powered by the 12 "Re-" actions,
plus consistent **Practice**, equals lasting **Value / transformation.**

The theme "MOVE" progresses clients from **awareness → control → power** over 3 months.
Movement quality and body awareness come **before** intensity.

## Stage 1 — "Uninformed Optimist": Awareness & Activation (Month 1)
- **Mindset:** optimistic but disconnected from how their body moves.
- **Focus:** foot/ground proprioception; activate pelvic floor + TVA for deep core
  stability; scapular protraction/retraction for shoulder & posture control.
- **Training:** foundational stability/mobility, range of movement, LISS cardio base.
- **Practice habits:** hydrate on waking; 5–10 min walk after meals; prioritize sleep.

## Stage 2 — "Informed Pessimist": Control & Coordination (Month 2)
- **Mindset:** now aware of limitations/asymmetries — temporary frustration, but necessary.
- **Focus:** hip mobility; shoulder IR/ER; breathing & bracing for intra-abdominal pressure;
  hand/head positioning for alignment & force transfer.
- **Training:** controlled speed, force/strength, add HIIT for work capacity.
- **Practice habits:** protein at every meal; 80/20 nutrition rule; moderate alcohol.

## Stage 3 — "Informed Optimist": Power & Performance (Month 3)
- **Mindset:** knowledge + control = grounded, informed confidence; ready to perform.
- **Focus:** rotation & anti-rotation strength; crossing the midline / integrated patterns;
  acceleration & deceleration mechanics; overall movement capacity.
- **Training:** work capacity/endurance under load, add SIT (sprint intervals) for peak power.
- **Practice habits:** track nutrition; consistent adherence; use data/feedback loops.

## The 12 "Re-" actions
Reinforce mindset & habits across all stages:

Reflect · Recommit · Reset Rituals · Rewire the Mindset · Relearn Movement ·
Record Your Metrics · Reevaluate Weekly · Reinforce with Culture · Refine Nutrition ·
Reoccurring Repetition · Rise through Resilience · Repeat.
