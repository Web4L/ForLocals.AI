# Newsletter Strategy

How Momentum's email newsletter earns trust and drives Physics 101 leads — without feeling
like a sales blast.

## The job of this newsletter
Social media earns the follow. The newsletter earns the **trust** that turns a follower into
a Physics 101 lead — and keeps current members from quietly drifting away. One useful idea
per issue, taught well, with a soft next step. We teach the *physics of movement* so people
believe results will last here when they don't elsewhere.

## Cadence
- **Default: monthly.** Sustainable to produce at a high quality bar, and frequent enough to
  stay top-of-mind without fatiguing the list.
- **Optional: biweekly** during a Physics 101 enrollment push (one teaching issue, one
  offer-forward issue).
- Send on a consistent day (e.g. the first Tuesday of the month, mid-morning). Consistency
  beats perfect timing.

## Audience segments
Keep the core teaching identical across segments; change only the opener, framing, and CTA.

| Segment | Who they are | Goal of the email | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Prospects** | On the list, haven't joined | Build belief; lower friction | "DM us **PHYSICS** to start" |
| **Members** | Currently training | Reinforce the method; retention | Apply the idea this week / refer a friend |
| **Quiet / lapsed** | Haven't opened in 60–90 days | Re-earn attention with value | One low-friction next step |

If the list isn't segmented yet, write to **prospects** as the default and add a one-line P.S.
for members.

## Issue anatomy (the repeatable structure)
Full template in `templates/issue-structure.md`. In short:
1. **Subject line + preview text** — curiosity or benefit, never clickbait.
2. **Opener** — a single relatable tension, in a real human voice.
3. **The one idea** — teach one principle (usually from the MOVE framework), with a concrete,
   visual example. *One idea, not five.*
4. **Proof** — a short member moment, result, or "aha."
5. **Soft CTA** — one next step toward Physics 101 (DM **PHYSICS**).
6. **Quick win** — a practical tip or PRACTICE habit they can use today.
7. **Sign-off** — warm, human, signed by a real person.

## Voice guardrails (from `../../brand/voice-tone.md`)
- **Do:** teach one clear idea; explain movement simply and visually; lead with *why*; be warm
  and credible.
- **Don't:** fitness-bro hype ("crush it," "beast mode"), shame, overpromise, or cram multiple
  asks into one email.

## Content pillars → newsletter themes
Drawn from `../../brand/content-pillars.md`:
- **Physics of Movement** → the core teaching issues (most common).
- **Physics 101 Experience** → "what actually happens / is it for me" issues.
- **Proof & Transformations** → member-story issues.
- **Culture & Expertise** → trainer-philosophy / behind-the-method issues.
- **Offers & Entry Points** → occasional offer-forward issues during a push.

## How we'll know it's working
Track per issue in `analytics/`: open rate, click rate, replies, and **PHYSICS DMs/mentions
attributable to the send**. The last one is the number that matters — opens are vanity, leads
are revenue. Aim to learn one thing per issue (best subject angle, best CTA placement) and
fold it back into the prompt library.
