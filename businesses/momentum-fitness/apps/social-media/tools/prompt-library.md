# Prompt Library for Momentum Fitness

Use these prompts with ChatGPT, Grok, or any LLM to generate high-quality, on-brand content fast.

## How to use this library

A blank AI chat knows **nothing** about Momentum, Physics 101, or the MOVE framework — if you don't give it that context, it will invent generic (and often wrong) details about your own programs.

**So: paste the _Master Context Block_ below as your first message in any new chat.** Then use any prompt from the sections that follow. If you start a fresh chat, paste the block again first.

> Quick rule of thumb: **new chat = paste the block first.** Everything after that stays on brand.

Drive qualified new leads into Physics 101. Lead capture happens via DM: invite

WHAT PHYSICS 101 IS
Physics 101 is our small-group coaching program — the low-risk entry point into
Momentum. It's where new clients learn the fundamentals of how to move well before
chasing intensity. Position it as the smart place to start, for total beginners AND
for people who are "already fit" but never learned the fundamentals. Common objections
to address: "Is this too basic for me?" and "I'm already in shape."

THE MOVE FRAMEWORK (our signature 3-month client curriculum)
Formula: Rx¹² + p = v  →  structured Prescription, powered by the 12 "Re-" actions,
plus consistent Practice, equals lasting Value/transformation.

  • STAGE 1 — "Uninformed Optimist": Awareness & Activation (Month 1)

    Focus: foot/ground proprioception; activate pelvic floor + TVA for deep core

  • STAGE 3 — "Informed Optimist": Power & Performance (Month 3)
    Mindset: knowledge + control = grounded, informed confidence; ready to perform.
    Focus: rotation & anti-rotation strength; crossing the midline / integrated

  Reflect · Recommit · Reset Rituals · Rewire the Mindset · Relearn Movement ·

WHO WE'RE TALKING TO
People tired of generic gym programs that don't deliver lasting results; people who
want to understand their bodies, not just sweat; beginners intimidated by big-box
gyms; and fit people who never built proper movement foundations.

VOICE & TONE
Educational, clear, confident, encouraging. Explain movement concepts in simple,
visual language anyone can grasp. Lead with insight and "why," not hype.
  DO: challenge common fitness beliefs; teach one clear idea per post; be warm and

  DON'T: use generic fitness-bro hype ("crush it," "no excuses," "beast mode"),
      shame the reader, over-promise, or be vague.

CALL TO ACTION
Soft, educational CTAs that point to Physics 101 via DM keyword (e.g.
"Want to start with the fundamentals? DM us PHYSICS"). Avoid hard-sell.

## Prompts

> Each prompt below assumes the Master Context Block is already loaded in the chat.

### Reel / Short Video Hooks
"Using the Momentum context above, generate 8 strong Reel hooks focused on Physics 101. Each should create curiosity or challenge a common fitness belief, and stay under 8 seconds when spoken aloud. Format as a numbered list."

### Educational Carousel Ideas
"Create 5 Instagram carousel ideas that each explain ONE principle from a specific stage of the MOVE framework (name the stage). Each idea needs a scroll-stopping slide-1 hook and clear educational payoff. Tie each back to why Physics 101 is the place to learn it."

### Physics 101 Specific Content
"Write 6 Instagram content ideas (mix of Reels and Carousels) designed to drive leads into Physics 101. For each, include: suggested hook, format, the core teaching point, and a soft DM-keyword CTA."

### Objection-Handling Content
"Physics 101 leads commonly think 'Is this too basic for me?' or 'I'm already fit, I don't need fundamentals.' Write 4 Instagram post concepts (hook + angle + CTA) that reframe these objections using the MOVE framework's 'awareness before intensity' principle."

### Ad Copy Variations
"Generate 5 variations of ad copy for a Physics 101 intro offer. Tone: educational and benefit-focused, not hype. Speak to people tired of generic gym programs. Each ends with a DM-keyword CTA."

### Caption Writing
"Write a strong Instagram caption for a Physics 101 Reel. Hook in the first 2 lines, then educational value tied to a MOVE framework concept, a touch of social proof, and a soft CTA to DM PHYSICS. Keep it conversational."

### Content Repurposing
"Take this longer piece of content about [topic] and turn it into 3 Instagram formats: 1 Reel concept, 1 carousel outline, and 1 Story series idea. Keep everything aligned to Momentum's educational voice and Physics 101 goal."

### Client Story / Testimonial Framing
"Turn this client result into 3 Instagram post concepts. Focus on what they learned through the MOVE framework (name the relevant stage) and how understanding it changed their training. Make it relatable for someone considering Physics 101."

### Weekly Content Planning
"Create a balanced 7-day Instagram content plan for Momentum Fitness. Prioritize Physics 101 lead generation. Mix the content pillars: educational (MOVE principles), proof/transformations, and culture/expertise. Suggest a format and a one-line hook for each day."

### MOVE Framework Deep-Dive Series
"Outline a 9-post Instagram series — 3 posts per MOVE stage (Awareness & Activation, Control & Coordination, Power & Performance). For each post give: the stage, one specific movement focus or PRACTICE habit from that stage, a hook, and the format. End the series with a CTA into Physics 101."
